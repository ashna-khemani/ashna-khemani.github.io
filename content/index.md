# Why is this called... \*checks notes\* methinks.md?
*methinks = "it appears to me", "I think"*

Whenever I work on large projects, one of the first things I do is make a file called `methinks.md`. Which is just a huge running to-do list, brain dump, and inner monologue of thoughts. I take little effort to go back and clean it up, or organize it, because it's useful to have a paper trail of what I was thinking. (It's organic... kind of like a garden...)

Having this imperfect trail I can revisit makes it easy to understand how I navigated every turn. I can review why I made certain decisions, record useful nuggets of research, collect info and lessons from past mistakes, point myself towards new ideas, or even see where the overall goal and root motivation completely changed. 

**Essentially a wiki (or a map) for my future self.**

I'm approaching this—I dunno, corner of the interwebs?—the same way. Just wording out thoughts as they come because it may or may not have useful insight down the line (either for me or for someone else). And if it isn't... well, at least one of us gets a good laugh out of it!
