# Why is this called... \*checks notes\* methinks.md?
Whenever I work on large projects, one of the first things I do is make a file called `methinks.md`. Which is just a huge braindump trashfire of thoughts! Its a nice way to leave a paper trail of what I was thinking and when to quickly get myself back on track after a break, record key info from past mistakes, and highlight valuable nuggets of information. Essentially like a mini wiki or treasure map for my future self.

I'm approaching this—I dunno, corner of the interwebs?—the same way. Just wording out thoughts as they come because it may or may not be useful down the line (either for me or for someone else). And if it isn't... well hopefully one of us gets a good laugh out of it!
