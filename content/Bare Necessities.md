If I'm gonna be somewhere for >3 months, this is what I think I need in order to not go insane.

* Left-brain work. A job, or school. Something that involves puzzles, problem solving, and analytical thought.
* My journal and a nice pen.
* Some kind of art (usually a coloring book and some pencils will do. Big bonus if there is a piano)
* A good stress-walk route and walkable weather
* Good book(s)
* Tea
* Good chocolate
* somewhere to go out and be stupid. and people to do it with
