# Character Classes

  

Let’s go a bit deeper into the character classes and how they work\!

* Barbarians are rage fueled melee combatants, who embraces their primal nature and calls upon it for strength.

* Bards are charismatic magic users who bring inspiration to their allies and dread to their foes. All while charming most anyone.

* Clerics are agents of divinity. Healers, wisemen and battle priests, the cleric through divine magic works the will of their god.

* Druids are the keepers of nature and through that become one with it. They draw their magic and their powers of shapeshifting from the essence of nature and seek to preserve its majesty.

* Fighters are men and women with a purpose, brave warriors who harness tactics and resilience to keep their edge and defeat their foes.

* Monks are defined by their willpower and strictly honed unarmed fighting ability. Taking advantage of a plethora of fighting styles and mystic abilities the ascetic monk should never be underestimated.

* Paladins are the the shining armoured knights and the grizzled old guard. These powerful men and women are so dedicated to the oaths they take, be it for an ideal or to a deity, it manifests itself in their ability to protect themselves and others.

* Rangers are people who live on the border between civilization and the wild. Whether from afar or near rangers are deadly adversaries and hunters are at home in nature where most would be uneasy.

* Rogues at the whimsical tricksters, the dexterous thieves, and the skilled assassins. Typically jacks of all trades the rogue relies on stealth and raw skill to keep them a step ahead of their enemies.

* Sorcerers have magic in their blood, naturally adept at using their pool of spells for their own means and channeling the nature of their bloodline are the hallmarks of a sorcerer.

* Warlocks are magic users who have squires their arcane powers through a pact with an otherworldly being. Calling upon the fountain of power their patron gives them both in and out of battle the warlock is a force to be reckoned with.

* Wizards are learned in the arcane arts the way a craftsman is in their trade. Through deep study and hard work a wizard can alter the fabric of reality around them.

* Artificer is a wizard that uses technology over raw magic

  

Each of these classes have their own subclasses that you can choose from (make sure to check the source). You can find them [here](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/#toc19).
