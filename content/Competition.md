am I competitive? what is healthy competition? I grew up in the Bay Area, which some see as hyper-competitive... I usually say that I didn't feel pressured to compete. Who knows, maybe I'm so disillusioned that I don't realize.

- Competition because that's just the nature of what you're doing (i.e., a robotics competition).
- Competition because you found someone very similar to you and you have the be the better one. Like a friendly rivalry
- Competition because you do not like someone else and you need to stay ahead for your own ego. 
- Competition with yourself... when is it good vs. not good to compare you to yourself? Is it always fair to present-you?
